# Tournament Host Guide & Setup Intake

A guide for organizers hosting a tournament with **Bert & Erne**. Two parts:

1. **Setup intake** — the questions we'll ask so we can build your tournament
   correctly. Most of these become part of the public event page.
2. **Things to consider** — optional touches that make the event better for
   players (and often pay for themselves).

> **Where this fits.** This is the *setup* step of the journey: an inquiry
> becomes a quote, the quote is accepted, and then **this guide + the intake
> form turn that "yes" into a fully configured tournament** ready to publish.
> (Engineering: see the "opportunity → tournament funnel" proposal in the
> backlog — this doc is the customer-facing half.)

## Part 1 — Setup intake (what we'll ask you)

Everything here either configures the tournament or appears on the public event
page. Anything you don't know yet is fine — we can fill it in as it firms up.

### The basics
- Tournament **name** and hosting **club / organization**
- **Primary contact** (name, email, phone) and a **backup contact**
- **Dates** (and a rain/backup date if outdoor), plus **start & end time each day**
- **Venue** name, full **address**, indoor/outdoor, and **number of courts**

### Events & format
- The **brackets** you want: skill divisions, age groups, gender
  (men's / women's / mixed / open), and singles / doubles
- **Which skill levels?** e.g. 2.5, 3.0, 3.5, 4.0, 4.5, 5.0+ — the divisions
  you want offered (and whether any are combined, e.g. "3.5–4.0")
- **Is it DUPR rated?** Do you want match results submitted to **DUPR**? If so,
  players will need their DUPR profiles / IDs, and we'll set the event up to
  report scores.
- **Expected size** and any **cap** per event (and whether you want a **waitlist**)
- **Format** per event (round robin, single/double elimination, pool → bracket)
- **Scoring** (e.g. games to 11, win by 2; rally vs. traditional; best-of)
- **A MoneyBall game?** An optional cash side-game / exhibition (players buy in,
  winner takes a pot) — a crowd favorite. Want us to include one, and on which
  day?

### Registration & pricing
- **Entry fee** (tournament-level) and **per-event fee**, and what each includes
- Registration **open** and **close** dates
- **Refund / cancellation policy** (this is shown to players at checkout)
- **Partner matching** — do you want a "needs a partner" board for doubles?

### Ball & equipment
- **What ball will you play with?** (brand / model — and whether it differs
  indoor vs. outdoor)
- **Who provides the balls** — host or players?

### Rules & policies *(these go in the event description)*
- **Any other club or facility rules** we should list
- **Where should spectators be?** (specific viewing areas, off-court rules)
- **Outside food** — allowed or not?
- **Outside alcohol** — allowed or not?
- **Can players film their own games?**
- Also worth stating: **footwear** (non-marking indoor), **pets**,
  **smoking / vaping**, and **warm-up court** availability

### Venue & logistics
- **Parking** — where to park, cost, overflow lot, accessible spots, and any
  load-in area. *Decide this early and let us put it in the description so
  players aren't surprised on the day.*
- **Restrooms**, **water / hydration** stations, **shade / tents**,
  **seating / bleachers**
- **Check-in** location and time; where players **stage** between matches

### Awards
- **Medals** — which places (typically 1st–3rd)? Provided by you or by us?
- **Additional prizes** for winners? (see *Things to consider*)

### On-site food, drink & vendors
- **Concessions / food trucks / coffee** — anything planned?
- **Vendor or sponsor tables** — allowed, and who?

### Media & communications
- **Event description** copy, your **logo**, and any **photos**
- A **photographer**, an **announcer**, or **live scores** on the day?
- **Social handles / hashtag**, and any **sponsors** to recognize

### Staffing & safety
- Who runs the **registration desk**, **court monitors**, and **referees / line
  judges**?
- **First aid / AED** location, **emergency plan**, and a **weather / rain plan**
- Any **insurance or waiver** requirements for your venue

## Part 2 — Things to consider

Optional, but these consistently lift the player experience — and several more
than pay for themselves.

### Prizes beyond medals
It's nice to give a prize alongside the medal, at least for **first place**.
With ~10 brackets and two winners each, that's about **20 items**. A quick,
reliable source: **local breweries**, who commonly donate **4-packs**. Local
**coffee roasters, gyms, sporting-goods shops, and restaurants** (gift cards,
swag) are good asks too.

### Raffles
- **Paddle raffle.** If you sell paddles, raffle **one per day** — it reliably
  **more than covers the cost** of the paddle.
- **Broader community raffle.** Hit up local companies for donations and run a
  larger raffle. **We're happy to manage the money and the ticket sales /
  collection for you.** One caveat: **we can't help solicit the gifts** — lining
  up donations is on the host.

### On-site vendors & experiences
- **Brewery sample table.** Invite a local brewer to set up a table (e.g. around
  **12 PM Saturday**) to hand out samples — if you're comfortable with it and
  it's permitted at your venue.
- Also popular: a **food truck at lunch**, a **coffee cart** in the morning, or a
  **paddle-demo table** from a brand.

### Player-experience touches
- **Water / hydration** stations and **shade** on hot days
- A **posted schedule** with estimated match times so players can plan
- **Music** and an **announcer** for the finals
- A simple **players' lounge** / shaded rest area

### Community & sponsors
- **Recognize sponsors** on signage, the event page, and announcements
- **Cross-promote** with nearby clubs to grow the field
