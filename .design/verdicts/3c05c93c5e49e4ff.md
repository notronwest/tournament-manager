## Findings

None above the line. The four strings are each accurate in every state the pricing code can
produce, render without overflow or truncation at 390, and the two that sit in the page header
now agree with each other where they did not before.

The three specific risks the change was suspected of were each checked and each cleared:

- **"$35 to enter" read as a surcharge on top of the $45.** It is not, and the claim it makes is
  the strongest one on the page. In `lib/pricing.ts` an override bracket is removed from the
  entry-fee ordering entirely (`computeLineItems`, the `.filter((t) => t.ev.event_fee_cents <= 0)`
  sort) and is charged `ev.event_fee_cents` flat in every branch — unaffected by tier, by pick
  order, and by `alreadyHasPaidEvent`. `tournaments.entry_fee_cents` was dropped
  (20260529160000_drop_legacy_fee_columns.sql), so there is no second tournament-level charge
  underneath it. Entering that bracket costs $35, always; entering it alone costs $35 and nothing
  else. "$X to enter" is a complete-admission construction, not a delta — a surcharge reading
  would need a "+", which the sibling line one row up (`+$30 each additional event`) does use. And
  the pill is a *more* invariant claim than the header's own "$45 to register", which changes to
  $55 on Dec 20 and to $30 for a second bracket. Nothing on the card is wrong (#6).
- **"to enter" against the header's "priced separately".** Measured: the two are never in one
  viewport. At 390 the header line sits at y≈460 and the pill at y≈1050; at 1280x800 with the pill
  centred the header line is at y=-9.8. So this is not two vocabularies colliding in a glance, it
  is a lede on one screenful and a label on the next — and the authoritative disclosure between
  them is the expanded pricing panel, where "BRACKETS PRICED SEPARATELY / Kids 18 & Under Doubles
  $35" puts the $35 in the same right-hand price column as $45 and $55. That panel reads as
  "instead of", unambiguously, and it is reachable from every tab. It is still three registers for
  one concept across four surfaces; see the notes (#10).
- **"(bracket price)" in the parenthetical series.** It parses. Register reads entry /
  included in entry / extra event / bracket price; Checkout reads registration / included in
  registration / additional event / bracket price. All four are noun phrases answering the same
  question — why this amount — and "bracket price" answers it, where "own price" left "own"
  without a referent (#6).

## Notes (non-blocking)

- **#6/#10, the weakest of the four: `RegisterPage.tsx:1608-1612`, "N brackets priced separately".**
  The surrounding parts each name a *charge* ("Entry", "1 included", "1 extra event"); this one
  names the *object* plus a passive clause, and it counts brackets inside a sentence whose lede
  counts events — "1 event (1 bracket priced separately)" in the single-pick case, which is the
  common one for a kids bracket. Something charge-shaped and matching the row label — "1 bracket
  price", "1 at its own price" — would sit in the series better. Not a block: it is accurate,
  grammatical (the previous verdict ca2361ce05238a8f flagged "1 own price" as a fragment, and that
  is fixed), and the event/bracket duality is pre-existing and loud elsewhere on this page (the
  "EVENTS" and "BRACKETS" tabs sit side by side, and the unchanged header line already says
  "brackets" directly under "includes 1 event").
- **Geometry of that same line, measured not guessed.** At 390 the content column is 350 px
  (`contentColStyle`, clamp resolves to 20 px gutters) and the summary card's inner width is
  318 px. A faithful replica in the live page: "3 events (Entry + 1 extra event + 1 bracket priced
  separately)" wraps to 2 lines (39 px vs 19.5 px), taking the card from 95.5 px to 115 px; the
  worst realistic string, "5 events (Entry + 1 included + 2 extra events + 2 brackets priced
  separately)", also stops at 2 lines. The `$125.00` total already wrapped onto its own row at 390
  under the old wording too (`flexWrap: "wrap"`), so nothing changes there. No overflow, no
  truncation — +19.5 px of card height is a nit.
- **The pill got smaller, which is the right direction (#13).** Measured in its own computed style
  (11 px / 600 / uppercase / 0.3 px tracking, `padding: 2px 8px`): "$35 to enter" is 99.8 px
  against "$35 · own price" at 115.9 px. It wraps to its own line under the 236 px title at 390 as
  it did before, and sits inline at 1280. Even a four-figure fee ("$1250 to enter", 113 px) stays
  narrower than the old three-figure-less version, so the wrap behaviour of the flex-wrap pill row
  — which also carries Registered / Pending payment / Awaiting partner — is strictly improved.
- **#14, measured on the surface it actually lands on.** Pill ink `rgb(20,24,31)` on cream
  `rgb(246,239,214)` = **15.4:1**. Panel heading, same ink on the gradient's tan end, >13:1 (the
  prior verdict's measurement, unchanged by this diff — the heading's colour, size and face are
  untouched, only its words). No horizontal scroll: `scrollWidth` 390 at 390 and 1280 at 1280, in
  both the collapsed and expanded pricing-panel states.
- **#10, the one that stays open and is bigger than this diff.** One concept is now named three
  ways across four surfaces: "priced separately" (header line + panel heading), "to enter" (card
  pill), "bracket price" (both line-item parentheticals). Each is right where it stands and none
  contradicts another, so it is not a block — but the product also already uses *entry* for the
  thing this price REPLACES ("included in entry", RegisterPage.tsx:2040; "Entry fee" on
  HomePage/Terms/Privacy) while `PartnerAcceptPage.tsx:443-445` labels this very
  `event_fee_cents` number "Entry fee". So "entry" means both charges, in opposite directions,
  depending on the screen. That drift pre-dates this change and this change does not widen it, but
  a single chosen phrase for the whole concept would retire it.
- **D-0018, the standard is silent here.** #19a requires a lede to name a per-item *control* in
  that control's own words; it says nothing about a per-item *label* whose explanatory lede sits
  on a different screenful. I graded the pill on the reading that a label is bound by #6
  (accurate, plain, no jargon) and #10 (does not contradict its neighbours), not by #19a. Worth
  writing down either way.
- Not rendered, and so not claimed: Register and Checkout (auth-gated), and the paid /
  pending-payment / awaiting-partner card states. The diff touches only label strings in those
  places — no conditional, no layout, no component — and the three strings' widths were measured
  against the real container widths above.
