   into it. Side by side on one screen: Dana's own pending entry gets the amber card, the
   PENDING PAYMENT pill, Change partner / Cancel Registration **and** the checkout bar; her
   child's identical pending entry gets one grey 12px line and no next move. This is #15's quote
   exactly — "there is no path for them to move it forward."

   Fix: include dependents' payable rows in `PendingPaymentsContext` (naming the registrant in
   the bar, as checkout already does) so the existing bar carries the child's entry and the
   correct total; and make the new card line say the entry is unpaid and link to checkout.
