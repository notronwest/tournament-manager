## Findings

1. **web/src/pages/public/PublicTournamentPage.tsx:2558 (the gate) → :2619–2633 (the button
   it lets through), against :1706–1708 and :1718–1721 → #12** — the card still offers
   **Register** on an event where no name in the chooser can register, and the form it opens
   refuses every one of them. The gate reads
   `me && !playerEligible && eligibleRegistrants.length === 0`, and `eligibleRegistrants`
   (:1706) filters on `checkEligibility` alone — age and gender. It never asks whether a
   registrant is **already in this event**, although `dependentEventIds`, the map that
   answers exactly that, is built in the same component and used twelve lines later (:1718)
   to refuse the submit. So the affordance is gated on eligibility and the submit is gated on
   eligibility *plus* already-registered — the mismatch is the dead end.

   Observed live on the seeded route at **390×844** and **1280×800**, Kids 18 & Under (Dana
   47, Maya 11 and already holding a pending entry in that bracket):

   - the card renders the filled red **REGISTER** CTA — its primary action;
   - tapping it opens the form defaulted to **Maya Harper** (:1770 picks the first *eligible*
     registrant), which immediately reads "**Maya is already registered** for this event.
     Pick someone else above, or ask the organizer if you need their entry changed." with
     **Save** disabled (71.5×45, `disabled=true`);
   - taking its advice and picking **Dana Harper (you)** reads "**You can't enter this event:
     needs age 18 or under.** Pick someone else above, or a different event." with Save
     disabled again. The chooser holds two names and both are refusals; nothing in the form
     can produce a registration.

   This is the case `docs/DESIGN_PREFERENCES.md` writes out verbatim — *"don't present that
   action and then reject it — remove or disable the control up front… proactive gating beats
   reactive gating"* — and the one it names as canonical (*"a player ineligible for an event
   sees no Register button"*). It is also attributable to this branch rather than inherited:
   for the **signed-in player's own** registration the card is correct, because `myStatus`
   short-circuits at :2505 into Change partner / Cancel Registration. This change introduces a
   second class of registrant — the dependent — whose registered state the card never reflects,
   so the one bracket the whole feature exists for is the one that dead-ends. At base this card
   showed Dana a plain "Not eligible: needs age 18 or under"; the button is new here.

   Note the direction of the bug: the same filter also means the button correctly appears when
   a parent has a *second* child who can still enter, so hiding it unconditionally is wrong.
   Fix: narrow `eligibleRegistrants` to registrants who are eligible **and** not already in
   this event (`dependentEventIds.get(r.player.id)?.has(event.id)` for a dependent, `myStatus`
   for the player), and — per the same convention's "replace, don't silently remove" guardrail
   — put the reason where the button was, naming the child ("Maya Harper is registered"),
   rather than leaving the card silent.
